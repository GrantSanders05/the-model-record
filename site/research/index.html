Best bets

Edge is the raw disagreement with the closing number. Worth is what that disagreement has historically been worth — only about an eighth of it shows up, so an eleven-point gap is a point and a half of real edge. Read Worth. Everything here is a hypothesis being tracked, not a bet known to be good.

The model's record

Every pick locked before kickoff and graded after, cut every way that could change the answer. No rate is shown without the number of decisions behind it and a 95% interval, because those are what decide whether it means anything.

Where the record comes from

A headline rate can hide a segment that is losing. These are the cuts most likely to be carrying — or draining — the whole thing.

Closing line value

Whether the number moved toward the pick after it was made. This is the only signal that means something before the win rate does — it needs dozens of bets, not thousands, because it does not depend on how the ball bounced.

Calibration

Actual margin regressed on predicted margin. A slope near 1 means the numbers are the right size; well under 1 means the model is directionally right and numerically overconfident, which is a scaling problem, not an ignorance problem.
