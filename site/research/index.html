Most load-bearing players

Who each team can least afford to lose, in points of spread. Computed by removing the player and re-rating all 138 teams — so it is the model's own answer, not a guess.
Moneyline EV strips the bookmaker's margin before comparing — raw implied probabilities sum to about 105%, so without that step nearly every game looks like value. Stakes are quarter-Kelly capped at 2% of bankroll. Spread disagreement is the weaker signal: the model's biggest disagreements have historically been its worst bets, not its best.
Closing-line value is the leading indicator of a real edge — it separates from noise months before a win rate does. History only exists from the day capture started, so this fills in as the season runs.
Re-prices every one of this team's games live, using the same arithmetic the engine uses: 2×(QB+RB+WR+OL+DL+LB+DB) + Coach/ST + WinPts − LossPts, then home total − away total + home-field. Nothing here writes back to the spreadsheet — it is a scratchpad for seeing what a grade change would do.
